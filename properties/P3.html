Capital (P3)

Property ID: P3
Label: Capital
Internal Key: capital
System: AWFLWIKIDATAWORLD
Status: Core / Geographic

WHAT IS P3?
Capital (P3) is a property that links a country or region to its capital city. It identifies the city that serves as the seat of government for that country or administrative region. P3 answers the question: "What is the capital of this place?"

HOW TO USE P3
P3 is used inside an item's statement list. You add it like any other property:
1. Open or create an item that represents a country or region (e.g., Q4 = Ghana).
2. Add a statement.
3. Select Capital (P3) as the property.
4. Enter the value — which must be an item representing a city (e.g., Q7 = Accra).
5. Save.
The value of P3 is always a Q-item (a city or town), never a string, number, or date.

WHERE P3 APPLIES
P3 applies primarily to:
- Country items → the capital city of that country.
- Administrative regions or states → the capital of that region.
- Historical countries or empires → their historical capital.
It does not apply to individuals, organizations, songs, or abstract concepts.

WHEN TO USE P3
Always use P3 when:
- You are documenting a country and want to state its capital.
- You are documenting a region or state within a country.
- You need AI or search systems to know the capital of a place.

Do NOT use P3 when:
- You want to state the country a city belongs to (use P2 country, or P11 located in).
- You want to state where a person was born (use P6 place of birth).
- You want to state a generic location (use P29 place).

EXAMPLES IN AEDTP WORLD CONTEXT
Example 1 — Country:
Item: Ghana (Q4)
P3 (capital) → Accra (Q7)

Example 2 — Region:
Item: Volta Region (Q8)
P3 (capital) → Ho (Q9)

Example 3 — Historical country:
Item: Gold Coast (Q10)
P3 (capital) → Accra (Q7)

WHY P3 MATTERS FOR AI
When an AI reads an item and sees P3 → Accra, it immediately knows:
- This item is a country or region.
- Its capital city is Accra.
- It can connect this to other geographic data, like population, language, and currency.
P3 is essential for geographic knowledge graphs and for answering questions like "What is the capital of Ghana?"

TECHNICAL NOTES
- Value type: Item (Q-ID) representing a city.
- Multiple values allowed: Rare, but can be multiple for countries with multiple capitals (e.g., South Africa has three).
- Required: Recommended for every country and administrative region item.
- Related properties: P2 country (to link a city back to its country), P11 located in (for broader location), P29 place (generic).

SUMMARY
P3 (Capital) answers: "What is the capital of this place?"
It is a fundamental geographic property in AWFLWIKIDATAWORLD.
Use it to link countries and regions to their capital cities.
