Date Of Birth (P5)

Property ID: P5
Label: Date Of Birth
Internal Key: date of birth
System: AWFLWIKIDATAWORLD
Status: Core / Biographical

WHAT IS P5?
Date Of Birth (P5) is a property that links a person to the date they were born. It records the specific day, month, and year a person came into existence. P5 answers the question: "When was this person born?"

HOW TO USE P5
P5 is used inside an item's statement list. You add it like any other property:
1. Open or create an item that represents a person (e.g., Q1 = Emmanuel Deliver Amable).
2. Add a statement.
3. Select Date Of Birth (P5) as the property.
4. Enter the value — which must be a date in the format YYYY-MM-DD (e.g., 2008-04-14).
5. Save.
The value of P5 is always a date, never a string, number, or Q-item.

WHERE P5 APPLIES
P5 applies primarily to:
- Person items → their birth date.
- Historical figures → their known birth date.
- Fictional characters → their birth date if established in canon.
It does not apply to organizations, places, songs, or abstract concepts.

WHEN TO USE P5
Always use P5 when:
- You are documenting a person and know their birth date.
- You want to sort or filter people by age or birth year.
- You need AI or search systems to calculate age or find people born on a certain date.

Do NOT use P5 when:
- You want to state the date an organization was founded (use P14 inception or P38 founding date).
- You want to state the date a creative work was published (use P14 inception or a similar property).
- You want to state a person's death date (there may be a separate property for that, or use P32 date with a qualifier).

EXAMPLES IN AEDTP WORLD CONTEXT
Example 1 — Person:
Item: Emmanuel Deliver Amable (Q1)
P5 (date of birth) → 2008-04-14

Example 2 — Person:
Item: Kwame Nkrumah (Q30)
P5 (date of birth) → 1909-09-21

Example 3 — Fictional character:
Item: Superman (Q40)
P5 (date of birth) → 1938-04-18 (first appearance date, sometimes used as birth)

WHY P5 MATTERS FOR AI
When an AI reads an item and sees P5 → 2008-04-14, it immediately knows:
- This person was born on April 14, 2008.
- It can calculate their current age.
- It can find other people born on the same day.
- It can place this person in a historical timeline.
P5 is essential for biographical knowledge and for temporal reasoning.

TECHNICAL NOTES
- Value type: Date (YYYY-MM-DD).
- Multiple values allowed: No — a person has only one birth date.
- Required: Recommended for every person item, but may be unknown for some historical figures.
- Related properties: P6 place of birth (where they were born), P32 date (generic date property), P14 inception (for organizations and works).

SUMMARY
P5 (Date Of Birth) answers: "When was this person born?"
It is a core biographical property in AWFLWIKIDATAWORLD.
Use it to link people to their birth dates for age calculation and historical context.
