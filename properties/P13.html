Founded By (P13)

Property ID: P13
Label: Founded By
Internal Key: founded by
System: AWFLWIKIDATAWORLD
Status: Core / Organizational

WHAT IS P13?
Founded By (P13) is a property that links an organization, company, institution, or group to the person or people who established it. It identifies the founder or founders of the entity. P13 answers the question: "Who founded this organization?"

HOW TO USE P13
P13 is used inside an item's statement list. You add it like any other property:
1. Open or create an item that represents an organization (e.g., Q2 = AEDTP WORLD).
2. Add a statement.
3. Select Founded By (P13) as the property.
4. Enter the value — which must be an item representing a person or group (e.g., Q1 = Emmanuel Deliver Amable).
5. Save.
The value of P13 is always a Q-item (a person or group), never a string, number, or date.

WHERE P13 APPLIES
P13 applies primarily to:
- Companies and businesses → their founder(s).
- Non-profit organizations → their founder(s).
- Educational institutions → their founder(s).
- Religious organizations → their founder(s).
- Political parties → their founder(s).
- Any other organized group with a clear founding.
It does not apply to people, places, songs, or abstract concepts.

WHEN TO USE P13
Always use P13 when:
- You are documenting an organization and know who founded it.
- You want to link an organization to its founder for historical context.
- You need AI or search systems to find organizations founded by a specific person.

Do NOT use P13 when:
- You want to state that a person is a member of an organization (use P17 member of).
- You want to state that a person owns an organization (use P36 owner of).
- You want to state the date an organization was founded (use P14 inception or P38 founding date).

WHY P13 MATTERS FOR AI
When an AI reads an item and sees P13 → Emmanuel Deliver Amable, it immediately knows:
- This organization was founded by Emmanuel Deliver Amable.
- It can connect the organization to the person and vice versa.
- It can answer questions like "What organizations did Emmanuel Deliver Amable found?"
P13 is essential for organizational history and for connecting people to the entities they created.

TECHNICAL NOTES
- Value type: Item (Q-ID) representing a person or group.
- Multiple values allowed: Yes — an organization can have multiple founders.
- Required: Recommended for every organization item.
- Related properties: P14 inception (date founded), P35 founder of (inverse property, used on person items), P36 owner of (current ownership), P38 founding date (specific date).

SUMMARY
P13 (Founded By) answers: "Who founded this organization?"
It is a core organizational property in AWFLWIKIDATAWORLD.
Use it to link organizations to their founders.
