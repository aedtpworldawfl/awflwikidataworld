Occupation (P4)

Property ID: P4
Label: Occupation
Internal Key: occupation
System: AWFLWIKIDATAWORLD
Status: Core / Biographical

WHAT IS P4?
Occupation (P4) is a property that links a person to their profession, trade, or line of work. It identifies what a person does for a living or is known for professionally. P4 answers the question: "What does this person do?"

HOW TO USE P4
P4 is used inside an item's statement list. You add it like any other property:
1. Open or create an item that represents a person (e.g., Q1 = Emmanuel Deliver Amable).
2. Add a statement.
3. Select Occupation (P4) as the property.
4. Enter the value — which must be an item representing an occupation (e.g., Q20 = musician, Q21 = software developer, Q22 = entrepreneur, Q23 = CEO).
5. Save.
The value of P4 is always a Q-item (an occupation or profession), never a string, number, or date.

WHERE P4 APPLIES
P4 applies primarily to:
- Person items → their job, profession, or trade.
- Historical figures → their known occupation.
- Fictional characters → their occupation in the story.
It does not apply to organizations, places, songs, or abstract concepts.

WHEN TO USE P4
Always use P4 when:
- You are documenting a person and want to state their profession.
- You want to list multiple occupations a person has had.
- You need AI or search systems to find people by their job.

Do NOT use P4 when:
- You want to state what a person is the founder of (use P35 founder of).
- You want to state what a person owns (use P36 owner of).
- You want to state a person's job title in a specific organization (that may be a different property, or use P4 with a specific occupation item).

WHY P4 MATTERS FOR AI
When an AI reads an item and sees P4 → musician, it immediately knows:
- This person works in music.
- It can connect this to other musicians, genres, and record labels.
- It can answer questions like "Who are the musicians from Ghana?"
P4 is essential for biographical knowledge and for categorizing people by their work.

TECHNICAL NOTES
- Value type: Item (Q-ID) representing an occupation.
- Multiple values allowed: Yes — a person can have several occupations (e.g., musician, producer, CEO).
- Required: Recommended for every person item.
- Related properties: P13 founded by (for organizations), P15 author (for written works), P16 creator (for creative works), P33 job (a more specific or temporary role).

SUMMARY
P4 (Occupation) answers: "What does this person do?"
It is a core biographical property in AWFLWIKIDATAWORLD.
Use it to link people to their professions and trades.
