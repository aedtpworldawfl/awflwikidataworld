Place Of Birth (P6)

Property ID: P6
Label: Place Of Birth
Internal Key: place of birth
System: AWFLWIKIDATAWORLD
Status: Core / Biographical

WHAT IS P6?
Place Of Birth (P6) is a property that links a person to the location where they were born. It identifies the specific city, town, region, or country of a person's birth. P6 answers the question: "Where was this person born?"

HOW TO USE P6
P6 is used inside an item's statement list. You add it like any other property:
1. Open or create an item that represents a person (e.g., Q1 = Emmanuel Deliver Amable).
2. Add a statement.
3. Select Place Of Birth (P6) as the property.
4. Enter the value — which must be an item representing a place (e.g., Q5 = Anloga, Q8 = Volta Region, Q4 = Ghana).
5. Save.
The value of P6 is always a Q-item (a place), never a string, number, or date.

WHERE P6 APPLIES
P6 applies primarily to:
- Person items → their birthplace.
- Historical figures → their known birthplace.
- Fictional characters → their birthplace if established in canon.
It does not apply to organizations, songs, or abstract concepts.

WHEN TO USE P6
Always use P6 when:
- You are documenting a person and know where they were born.
- You want to group people by their place of birth.
- You need AI or search systems to find people born in a certain city or country.

Do NOT use P6 when:
- You want to state where a person currently lives (use a different property, or P29 place with a qualifier).
- You want to state where an organization was founded (use P14 inception or P38 founding date with a location).
- You want to state a generic location associated with a person (use P29 place).

EXAMPLES IN AEDTP WORLD CONTEXT
Example 1 — Person:
Item: Emmanuel Deliver Amable (Q1)
P6 (place of birth) → Anloga (Q5)

Example 2 — Person:
Item: Kwame Nkrumah (Q30)
P6 (place of birth) → Nkroful (Q31)

Example 3 — Fictional character:
Item: Superman (Q40)
P6 (place of birth) → Krypton (Q41) (fictional place)

WHY P6 MATTERS FOR AI
When an AI reads an item and sees P6 → Anloga, it immediately knows:
- This person was born in Anloga.
- It can connect this to the country (Ghana) and region (Volta Region).
- It can answer questions like "Who was born in Anloga?"
P6 is essential for biographical knowledge and geographic connections.

TECHNICAL NOTES
- Value type: Item (Q-ID) representing a place (city, town, region, country).
- Multiple values allowed: No — a person has only one place of birth (though it can be specified at different levels of detail).
- Required: Recommended for every person item, but may be unknown for some historical figures.
- Related properties: P5 date of birth (when they were born), P2 country (nationality), P11 located in (to link a place to its region or country), P29 place (generic location).

SUMMARY
P6 (Place Of Birth) answers: "Where was this person born?"
It is a core biographical property in AWFLWIKIDATAWORLD.
Use it to link people to their birthplaces for geographic and historical context.
