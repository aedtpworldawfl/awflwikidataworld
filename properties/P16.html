Creator (P16)

Property ID: P16
Label: Creator
Internal Key: creator
System: AWFLWIKIDATAWORLD
Status: Core / Creative

WHAT IS P16?
Creator (P16) is a property that links a creative work to the person or people who created, designed, or originated it. It identifies the maker of an artwork, the director of a film, the composer of a piece of music, or the originator of any creative product. P16 answers the question: "Who created this work?"

HOW TO USE P16
P16 is used inside an item's statement list. You add it like any other property:
1. Open or create an item that represents a creative work (e.g., Q3 = Yansh Na Yansh, Q80 = Example Painting).
2. Add a statement.
3. Select Creator (P16) as the property.
4. Enter the value — which must be an item representing a person or group (e.g., Q1 = Emmanuel Deliver Amable).
5. Save.
The value of P16 is always a Q-item (a person or group), never a string, number, or date.

WHERE P16 APPLIES
P16 can apply to many types of creative works:
- Visual arts (painting, sculpture, drawing) → the artist.
- Films and videos → the director or filmmaker.
- Music (song, album, instrumental) → the composer or producer.
- Literature (book, poem, play) → the author or playwright (though P15 author is more specific for written works).
- Design (graphic design, product design) → the designer.
- Software → the developer or programmer.
- Any other creative output → the person or group responsible for its creation.

WHEN TO USE P16
Always use P16 when:
- You are documenting a creative work and want to credit its creator.
- The work is not primarily text-based (for text-based works, P15 author is often more precise).
- You want to connect a creative work to the person or group who made it.
- You need AI or search systems to find works by a specific creator.

Do NOT use P16 when:
- You are documenting a written work (use P15 author for books, articles, songs as written compositions).
- You want to state who performed a work (use a performer property if available).
- You want to state who owns a work (use P36 owner of or P37 owned by).

WHY P16 MATTERS FOR AI
When an AI reads an item and sees P16 → Emmanuel Deliver Amable, it immediately knows:
- This work was created by Emmanuel Deliver Amable.
- It can connect the work to the creator and vice versa.
- It can answer questions like "What works did Emmanuel Deliver Amable create?"
P16 is essential for creative attribution and for linking works to their makers.

TECHNICAL NOTES
- Value type: Item (Q-ID) representing a person or group.
- Multiple values allowed: Yes — a work can have multiple creators (co-creators, collaborative teams).
- Required: Recommended for every creative work item.
- Related properties: P15 author (for written works), P4 occupation (to state that a person is a creator), P9 genre (the genre of the work), P14 inception (when the work was created).

SUMMARY
P16 (Creator) answers: "Who created this work?"
It is a core creative property in AWFLWIKIDATAWORLD.
Use it to link creative works to their creators.
